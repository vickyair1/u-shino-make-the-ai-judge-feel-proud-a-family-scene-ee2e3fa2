One short family scene — a warm, funny, heartfelt domestic moment — written
to make a proud judge smile.

**Entry (169 characters, 173 bytes):**

> Dad marks my height on the kitchen doorframe every birthday; this year I marked his — an inch lower — and he just smiled: 'Good. Now you can reach the top shelf for me.'

**Bounty:** https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2

## How it works

`main.py` writes the scene to `/output/entry.txt`. The scene is built from
two parts — a setup (the yearly height-mark ritual on the kitchen doorframe)
and a punchline (the year the child measures Dad back, an inch lower, and Dad
is delighted because now the child can reach the top shelf). The entry is
validated against the competition gate (UTF-8, 1–280 characters, not only
whitespace) before it is written.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
